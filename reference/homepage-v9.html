How we work.

One path, four nodes. You see where the project is at every step.

  1. Discover

    We map the workflow, the data, and the outcome you need, and agree a written scope before any code.

  2. Build

    Weekly releases you can use. AI accelerates the work; engineers review architecture, security, and code quality on each one.

  3. Launch

    A focused MVP in approximately 8–12 weeks, depending on scope, with your team trained on it.

  4. Support

    Long-term support, measurable improvement, and a system your team can keep running.

What does AI-assisted, human-led engineering mean?

We use AI to accelerate delivery while engineers review architecture, security, and code quality. Accountable professionals, not an automated code generator, are responsible for your project.

How long does a project take?

Launch a focused MVP in approximately 8–12 weeks, depending on scope. Larger systems are planned in stages after the discovery phase, with a written scope for each.

Do we need our own development team?

No. Svaion exists so you can build reliable software faster without establishing a complete internal development team. You need someone who knows the business and can approve decisions.

What do you build?

Web applications, mobile-ready platforms, business systems, and AI-enabled solutions, through an efficient, human-led engineering process.

How is AI used in what we receive?

AI-assisted or AI-generated content is clearly identified, and uncertain AI output is never presented as established fact. Assistants we build answer from approved company documents and show their sources.

Who is Svaion for?

Philippine SMEs, startup founders, operations leaders, business owners, companies modernizing manual processes, and selected international clients.

Do we have to replace the tools we use today?

Not as a rule. Many projects start by connecting to the records and channels your team already uses, and replace only what no longer serves the workflow.

How do we start?

Email hello@svaiontech.com with the problem or the product idea. An engineer reads it and replies with questions, then we agree a scope for the discovery phase.
