# Offering

What Svaion builds, the kinds of clients it serves, and what every engagement includes.

## Language

**Service line**:
One of the four kinds of system Svaion builds: web applications, mobile-ready platforms, business systems, AI-enabled solutions.
_Avoid_: product, package, offering (for a single item)

**Audience segment**:
A kind of client Svaion serves, such as Philippine SMEs or operations leaders.
_Avoid_: persona, target market, ICP

**Engagement**:
The work Svaion does for one client, from the first conversation to long-term support.
_Avoid_: contract, deal, account

**Engagement commitment**:
Something every engagement includes, such as a named lead engineer or long-term support.
_Avoid_: feature, perk, guarantee

**Focused MVP**:
The first release of a client's product that people can use, scoped to land inside the MVP window.
_Avoid_: prototype, pilot, v1, POC

**MVP window**:
Approximately 8–12 weeks, depending on scope; always stated as an approximate range, never as a deadline.
_Avoid_: timeline, deadline, SLA, delivery date

**Staged plan**:
How a system too large for one MVP window is delivered: in stages after discovery, each with its own written scope.
_Avoid_: roadmap, phases
