# Delivery

How an engagement runs, and the rule that every piece of AI-assisted work has a person who answers for it.

## The path

**Delivery stage**:
One of four stages every engagement passes through, in order: Discover, Build, Launch, Support.
_Avoid_: phase, sprint, milestone

**Written scope**:
The agreed description of what will be built, settled in Discover before any code.
_Avoid_: spec, requirements doc, SOW

**Release**:
A usable increment shipped during Build, reviewed by engineers for architecture, security, and code quality.
_Avoid_: deploy, drop, version

**Lead engineer**:
The named engineer who decides on architecture and is accountable for the engagement.
_Avoid_: PM, account manager, tech lead

## Accountability

**AI-assisted work**:
Work an AI tool drafted that a named person reviewed before it counted; always identified as such.
_Avoid_: AI-generated (for reviewed work), automated, autonomous

**Accountability rule**:
A pairing of something the AI does with the person who answers for it.
_Avoid_: policy, guardrail, control

**Accountable person**:
The human named in an accountability rule; never the AI itself.
_Avoid_: owner, approver, human in the loop
