# Showcase

Fictional, end-to-end examples of AI-assisted, human-led work inside a client's business.

## Language

**Scenario**:
An illustrative walk-through of one piece of AI-assisted work, from request to sign-off; its names and figures are fictional.
_Avoid_: case study, testimonial, client story

**Customer**:
A client's own customer or staff member, the person who makes the request in a scenario.
_Avoid_: client (that is the business that engages Svaion), user

**Request**:
What the customer asks for, in their own words.

**Assistant draft**:
The AI's proposed answer or action, carrying its disclosure.
_Avoid_: AI answer, bot reply, response

**Disclosure**:
The label on an assistant draft that says it is AI-assisted and, where there is one, names its source.
_Avoid_: disclaimer, footnote

**Source**:
The approved company document an assistant draft relies on.

**Sign-off**:
The named person's decision that makes an assistant draft count: approving, accepting, or reviewing it.
_Avoid_: approval (too narrow), confirmation
