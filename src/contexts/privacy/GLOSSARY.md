# Privacy

What personal data Svaion handles, why, for how long, who else handles it, and the rights people have over it. The words are those of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

## Roles

**Data subject**:
A person whose personal data Svaion handles: anyone who visits the website or writes to Svaion, whether or not they work for a client.
_Avoid_: user, lead, contact (as a person)

**Personal information controller**:
The organization that decides what personal data is handled and why. For Svaion, that is its legal name.
_Avoid_: data owner

**Personal information processor**:
A provider that handles personal data for Svaion, under contract and only on Svaion's instructions: hosting, email, and AI tools.
_Avoid_: vendor, partner, third party (a third party is anyone else)

**Data Protection Officer**:
The person accountable for how Svaion complies with the Act, reached through the privacy contact channel.
_Avoid_: privacy team

**National Privacy Commission**:
The regulator that enforces the Act and hears complaints from data subjects.

## Personal data

**Personal data**:
Information that identifies a person, on its own or combined with other information.
_Avoid_: user data, PII

**Processing activity**:
One way Svaion comes to hold personal data, with its own personal data, purposes, lawful bases, retention, and processors: a website visit or an email.

**Purpose**:
Why a processing activity happens. Personal data is used only for the purposes the privacy notice names.

**Lawful basis**:
The ground the Act allows for a processing activity: steps a data subject asks for before a contract, the contract itself, or a stated legitimate interest.
_Avoid_: permission, consent (when consent is not the basis)

**Retention**:
How long personal data is kept before it is deleted.

**Inquiry**:
A message from someone who has not engaged Svaion. It becomes an engagement or is deleted when its retention runs out.
_Avoid_: lead, prospect

**Request log**:
The hosting provider's record of each page request: IP address, browser, page, and time.

**Website collection**:
Anything the website itself stores on a visitor's device or sends to another company: cookies, analytics, forms, and third-party scripts or fonts. All of it must be declared in the privacy notice.
_Avoid_: tracking (it says nothing about which kind)

**AI tool**:
A model-based service Svaion uses to draft or analyze. It is a personal information processor, and a named person reviews what it produces.

## Rights

**Data subject right**:
A right the Act gives every data subject: to be informed, to object, to access, to rectification, to erasure or blocking, to damages, to data portability, and to file a complaint.

**Privacy notice**:
The public statement of the processing activities, who handles the data, how long it is kept, and the data subject rights.
_Avoid_: privacy policy, terms
