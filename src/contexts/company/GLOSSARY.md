# Company

Who Svaion is, the names it may use, the words it has approved, and how a prospective client reaches it.

## Names

**Svaion**:
The company's short name, used in running text and in the wordmark.
_Avoid_: SVT, Svaion Tech

**Svaion Technologies**:
The name the company presents under on its website.

**Legal name**:
SVAION TECHNOLOGIES, INC., a proposed corporate name until the SEC approves it.
_Avoid_: company name, registered name (while proposed)

**Registration status**:
Whether the legal name is still proposed or has been approved by the SEC.

**Registration notice**:
The line that must accompany the legal name while its registration status is proposed.

## Approved words

**Tagline**:
"Intelligence, switched on.", used verbatim, at most once on a page, never inside the logo.
_Avoid_: slogan, motto

**Approved message**:
Wording the CEO and COO have signed off (the headline, the subheadline, the timing message); used verbatim.
_Avoid_: copy, marketing text

**Descriptor**:
The one-line description of what Svaion is, used where the company introduces itself in brief.

## Reaching Svaion

**Contact channel**:
A mailbox on the company domain with one purpose: general, sales, support, or privacy.
_Avoid_: inbox, email (as a concept), contact form

**Client**:
A business that engages Svaion.
_Avoid_: customer, account, user

**Social handle**:
The company's name on social networks, @svaiontech.
